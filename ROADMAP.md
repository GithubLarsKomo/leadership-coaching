# ROADMAP

## 0.1 — Auditable timed core
Definitions, scorecards, sessions, locked rounds, server timing, attempts, versioned answers, raw handoff and export.

## 0.2 — Human assessment workflow
Coach assessment editor, assessment revisions, development-state projection, retry/retest and evidence-centric dashboard.

## 0.3 — LLM-assisted workflow
Provider-neutral structured generation, manual approval gate, bootstrap/assessment/next-round prompts, provenance and provider/model comparison fixtures.

## 0.4 — PES/SGL coaching program
Baseline block, training loops, transfer tests, robustness tests, final assessment and readiness report.

## 0.5 — Multi-framework support
Reusable role/scorecard packs, additional leadership programs, pack authoring validation and import/export.

## 1.0 — Production hardening
Authorization matrix, retention/deletion policy, backup/restore test, accessibility acceptance, threat model, observability, rate limits and release runbook.
